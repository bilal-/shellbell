## Authorized access and your responsibilities

Only connect computers and sessions you own or have permission to access.
Pairing grants terminal access with the permissions of the computer's operating
system user. Commands can read, change or delete files and access other systems

for each command.

You are responsible for the commands you send, the devices you authorize, and
your compliance with the rules that apply to those computers and data. Protect

Only choose a custom relay if you know who operates it and understand its setup
and privacy practices. Its operator receives connection and routing metadata and
may log or retain it. With unmodified Shellbell clients, terminal decryption keys
remain on the paired devices. The relay can still delay or block connections.
Shellbell cannot verify another operator's configuration, logs, backups or
retention. Changing relays does not delete data held by the old operator.
